# How to open and read the F1 Title Race dashboard

*A two-minute guide — no technical knowledge needed.*

## Opening it

The dashboard is a normal web page. Open this address in any browser, on any
device:

**https://simon-ioai.github.io/f1-2026-dashboard/**

That's it. There is nothing to install, no account, no login. Bookmark it, or
better:

### Put it on your phone's home screen (recommended)

Then it opens like an app with one tap — perfect for Monday-morning coffee.

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the address above → tap the Share button (the
  square with an arrow) → scroll down → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the address above → tap the ⋮ menu (top right) →
  **Add to Home screen** → **Add**.

## When does it update?

By itself — you never need to do anything:

- **Every day around 14:00 Danish time:** fresh title-race probabilities from
  the betting market.
- **Monday mornings after a race weekend:** full results, standings, and all
  the analysis panels.

If a page you opened earlier looks stale, just pull down (or refresh) to
reload it.

## Reading it

The dashboard explains itself: at the very top there is a panel called
**"New here? What this dashboard is and how it works"** — tap it to expand a
plain-language tour of every section, and tap again to close it.

The one number to know: the big percentages at the top are **the betting
market's opinion of each driver's title chances** — 76% means the market
thinks that driver becomes champion 76 times out of 100. Everything below is
different ways of looking at why.

## Sharing it

Send anyone the same address — it's a public page. They don't need anything
from you to view it.

## If something looks wrong

An amber ⚠ badge near the top means one of the data sources was temporarily
down; the dashboard shows the last good data instead of guessing, and heals
itself on the next update. If the badge persists for days, that's the moment
to ask a technical friend (or Claude) to look at the repository's Actions tab.
